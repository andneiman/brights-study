Pay for Brights with your Arizona ESA in ClassWallet

Arizona ESA funds are spent through ClassWallet. You don’t pay out of pocket: request an invoice from us, upload it in ClassWallet with Direct Pay, and the payment goes from your ESA straight to Brights.

Good to know

What our invoice includes

  • Student’s full name, as on the ESA account
  • Brights as the provider, with our contact details
  • The course and what it includes
  • Invoice number and date
  • Itemized price and the total
  • One invoice per student, never a family package

Before you submit

  • Brights is paid through Direct Pay with our invoice, so you never pay out of pocket
  • The amount must match the invoice total
  • Orders over $2,000 are reviewed by hand at ADE, so approval can take longer
  • To cancel an order, call ClassWallet at (877) 969-5536. Cancellation is only possible within 36 hours of submitting
