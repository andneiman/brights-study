How the course works

Personalized for your child

The course starts with your student's interests, school subjects, goals and experience with AI. Examples, practice and the final study plan are built around them. The learning objectives stay the same.

Studies independently

Short explanations, examples and hands-on practice in every unit. No live classes and no fixed schedule: about 3 units per study day, at your student's own pace.

Parent Mode included

The same units at an adult level, so you can see how your child learns and uses AI. Follow progress in the parent dashboard.

What is included

33 units with practice on the student's own subjects, quizzes after each unit, a personal two-week study plan and the student's own rules for learning with AI.

How to start

Choose the course, fill in a short parent questionnaire and let your student answer a few questions. We deliver the personalized course in 5 working days.

Price and access

$69 for a 2-week course, one package per student. Your family keeps access after the two weeks. For homeschool, microschool and ESA families.
