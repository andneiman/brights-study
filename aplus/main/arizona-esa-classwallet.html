Arizona ESA

Pay with your Arizona ESA in ClassWallet

Arizona ESA funds are spent through ClassWallet. You don’t pay out of pocket: create your invoice below, upload it in ClassWallet with Direct Pay, and the payment goes from your ESA straight to Brights.

Get my invoice → See the steps
  1. 1

    Open ClassWallet and click “Pay”

    Sign in to the Arizona ESA portal and go to ClassWallet. In the Direct Pay box, click PAY.

    You can also open the menu (☰) and choose Pay Vendor.

    More than one child on ESA? Switch to the right child’s account first, and repeat the steps for each child.

  2. 2

    Find A+ Brights and click “PAY”

    In “Who do you want to pay?”, type A+ Brights. You can also search by category or tag. Next to A+ Brights, click PAY.

  3. 3

    Enter the amount from your invoice

    In Enter Payment Amount, type the total from your Brights invoice, exactly. Then click NEXT.

    Get invoice ↓
  4. 4

    Upload your invoice

    Drag the invoice PDF into the box, or click BROWSE FILES. Then click NEXT.

    Don’t have the invoice yet? Create it in a minute, it downloads as a PDF right away.

    Get invoice ↓

    ClassWallet accepts PDF, JPEG or PNG, up to 10 MB and 5 pages.

  5. 5

    Choose the purse and expense category

    Tick your Arizona ESA purse. It covers the full total.

    Then choose the expense category that fits your order. Click NEXT.

  6. 6

    Add a comment and the invoice number

    In Comments, write what you’re paying for, for example: A+ Brights Learn How to Learn Smart (with AI).

    In Invoice or Quote Number, enter the number from your invoice (it looks like BR-AZ-111026-1). Click NEXT.

  7. 7

    Review and submit

    Check the vendor, total, category and invoice number, then click NEXT. You’ll see “Submitted!”, and ClassWallet emails you when the order is approved.

What happens next

  1. ClassWallet approves the payment. You’ll get an email from ClassWallet once the order is approved.
  2. We send you a few questions. About you and your child, so we can personalize the course.
  3. Your child’s personalized course arrives. We prepare it and send it to you within 5 days.
