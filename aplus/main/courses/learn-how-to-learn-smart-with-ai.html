Inside the course

Short lessons, real practice

Personalized for your child

At the start, the course collects your student's interests, school subjects, goals and current experience with AI. Examples, practice tasks and the final study plan are built around them, while the learning objectives stay the same.

Parent Mode included

Parent Mode covers the same units at an adult level, so you can see how your child learns and uses AI and support good study habits at home.

Independent learning for homeschool students

Each unit gives a short explanation, examples and hands-on practice. There are no live classes and no fixed schedule. The course is designed for about 3 units per study day, and your student can pause, go back over a unit or move ahead. Parents can follow progress in the parent dashboard.

How it works

Choose the course, complete the parent questionnaire and let your student answer a few questions about their interests, subjects and goals. We build the personalized version in 5 working days, and the finished course appears in your account.

What is included

  • 33 units with explanations, examples and hands-on practice on the student's own subjects
  • Quizzes and practice tasks after each unit
  • A personal two-week study plan and the student's own rules for learning with AI
  • Parent Mode and parent progress reporting: completion, results and next steps

Who it is for

Homeschooling and microschool families, students using education savings account (ESA) funds where the program allows, and any student in grades 4–12 who wants to study more effectively and use AI honestly and safely.

Access and pricing

2-week course: $69. The course is designed for two weeks, and your family keeps access to it afterwards. One package per student.
